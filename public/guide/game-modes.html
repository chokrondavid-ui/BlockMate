All Blockmate game modes

Blockmate has five ways to play. Here is what each one is for, how long a game lasts, and what it changes for your rating and your Blockcoins.

Play free now →

Solo vs bots

Three bots with different styles: Rookie (rated 800) plays almost at random and is perfect to learn; Pro (1200) always takes the most points it can; Master (1600) reads your next move and tries to block you. In Ranked, your Elo moves and you can win or lose Blockcoins earned by playing. In Practice, nothing is at risk and you can set a score target (first to 100, 200 or 500 points) or a clock.

Online ranked

Matchmaking finds a real player near your rating. Choose the speed: 2:30, 5:00 or 7:30 on each player's clock. Classic rules apply: the game ends when someone is stuck, and the highest score wins. A win gives Elo and Blockcoins; a loss costs some.

Play a friend

Create a game link and send it, or challenge a friend who is online. You choose the speed, the win condition and an optional bet of Blockcoins earned by playing. Friend games never change your Elo.

Same screen

Two players take turns on one phone or computer. Perfect for a break, a party or teaching a friend.

Daily challenge

A solo run with the same pieces for everyone in the world, renewed every day at midnight (Paris time). One attempt, one leaderboard, and up to 60 Blockcoins.

Rewards for everyone

A daily login reward grows over a 7-day streak. Blockcoins unlock skins, glows, avatars and lobby themes. These items only change how the game looks, never how it plays.

FAQ

Which mode should I start with?

Practice against the Rookie bot, then the Pro bot, before trying ranked.

How long is a game?

Between 3 and 15 minutes depending on the speed you choose.
