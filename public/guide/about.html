About Blockmate

Blockmate is an independent game, designed and developed in France. It started with a simple question: block puzzles are fun alone, so what happens when two people share the same board?

Who makes it

Blockmate is made by David Chokron, a business student at EDHEC Business School and an independent web developer based in Nice, France. It is developed and run as a small independent business, with no publisher and no investors.

What we care about

  • Fair play: every ranked game is checked on the server, so results can't be faked.
  • Free for everyone: every mode is free. Optional cosmetic items never change how the game plays.
  • Respect: the in-game chat filters insults and threats, and players can mute anyone.
  • Privacy: no account is needed to play, and we collect only what the game needs. See our privacy policy.

How the game is built

Blockmate runs entirely in the browser, with no download. The game rules run both on your device and on our server, which replays every ranked game to make sure it is valid. Online matches are played live over a secure connection.

FAQ

Is Blockmate made by a big studio?

No. It is an independent game made by one developer in France.

How is Blockmate funded?

By optional cosmetic purchases and, in the future, advertising. The game itself stays free.
