The daily block puzzle challenge

Every day at midnight (Paris time), Blockmate generates a new run of pieces. Everyone in the world gets exactly the same ones. You have one attempt to score as many points as possible before nothing fits.

Play free now →

How it works

  1. Open Blockmate and tap Daily challenge.
  2. Place pieces on an empty 8×8 board and clear lines to score. Combos multiply your points.
  3. When no piece fits, your run is over. Your score goes on today's leaderboard.
  4. Share your result and see if your friends can beat it.

Rewards

You earn 1 Blockcoin for every 10 points, up to 60 per day. Spend them on skins, glows, avatars and lobby themes.

Why play every day

Because everyone has the same pieces, the daily challenge is the purest test of skill: no luck in the draw, only your choices. It is also the best training for duels, as you learn which shapes leave the board open.

FAQ

When does the challenge reset?

Every day at midnight, Paris time.

Can I retry?

No, you get one attempt per day. That keeps the leaderboard fair.
