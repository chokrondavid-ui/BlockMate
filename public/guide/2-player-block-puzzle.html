The 2 player block puzzle

Block puzzles are usually a solo game. Blockmate turns them into a duel: two players share one board, and each piece you place can open or ruin your opponent's next move.

Play free now →

Why a duel changes everything

In a classic block puzzle you only fight the board. In Blockmate you also fight a person. The pieces you don't use become your rival's hand, you see their next options, and you can block them to end the game. It plays like chess with blocks: easy to learn, hard to master.

Ways to play with two players

  • Against a friend online: create a game link in one click and send it on WhatsApp, Messenger or Discord. Your friend plays in their browser, no account needed.
  • On the same screen: hand the phone back and forth, perfect for a break or a bus ride.
  • Against a stranger: ranked matchmaking finds someone near your level in seconds.
  • Against a bot: three levels when nobody is around.

Short, fair games

Each player has a chess-style clock: 2:30, 5:00 or 7:30. You can also play to a score target: first to 100, 200 or 500 points wins. Games last between 3 and 15 minutes.

Climb the ranking

Ranked games use an Elo rating, like chess. Start in Bronze and climb to Master, and see where you stand in your country and worldwide.
