How to play Blockmate

Blockmate is a block puzzle for two. You and your rival share the same 8×8 board and take turns placing pieces. Every line you clear scores points, and every piece you place also changes what your opponent can do next.

Play free now →

The rules in 30 seconds

  1. Each turn you get three pieces and place one of them anywhere it fits on the shared board.
  2. Fill a full row or column to clear it and score points. Clearing several lines with one piece scores much more.
  3. Clear lines on consecutive turns to build a combo multiplier.
  4. The two pieces you didn't use go to your rival, with one new piece. You always see your rival's next choices.
  5. The game ends when someone can't place any piece. That player's rival gets a +25 block bonus, and the highest score wins.

10 tips to win more

  1. Keep the centre of the board open: it is where big pieces fit.
  2. Before you place, look at your rival's next choices. Don't leave a line one piece away from being cleared by them.
  3. Set up two lines at once rather than one: 30 points beats two times 10.
  4. Protect your combo. A small clear that keeps the multiplier alive is often worth more than a big piece.
  5. Give your rival the pieces that fit worst. The two you don't play are their next hand.
  6. Late in the game, think about blocking: if your rival can't place anything, you get +25.
  7. Long 1×5 bars are rescue pieces. Keep a free row or column for them.
  8. Watch the clock in online games: running out of time loses the game.
  9. Practice against the Pro bot with a score target before trying ranked.
  10. Play the daily challenge every day: it's the fastest way to learn which shapes fit together.
